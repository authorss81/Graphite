Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Graphite Notes ("the app", "we", "us") is a local-first note-taking application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Notes & Content: Stored locally on your device (localStorage / IndexedDB). Optionally synced to Supabase if you create an account.
  • Account Email: Only if you sign up for cloud sync. Used only for authentication.
  • Usage Data: We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or behavioural data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (GAID, IDFA).
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you sign in with an email address, your notes are synced to Supabase (supabase.com), a GDPR-compliant backend. You can delete your account and all cloud data at any time from within the app.

4. End-to-End Encryption

Graphite offers optional AES-256-GCM end-to-end encryption for individual notes. When enabled, your note content is encrypted on your device before being sent to the server. We cannot read encrypted notes.

5. Camera & File Access

The app may request camera or file-picker access to insert photos or attachments into your notes. These files are stored locally and never uploaded without your explicit action.

6. Data Retention

Local data is retained until you uninstall the app. Cloud data is retained until you delete your account. You can export all your notes as Markdown or HTML at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Graphite Notes is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Contact us at: privacy@graphite-notes.app
